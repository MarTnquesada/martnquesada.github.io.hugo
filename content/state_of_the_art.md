**Last updated: September 2027**

Sometimes you are working on a machine learning-adjacent task but it has been a while since you
last looked up what are the best openly available models for it. It sure happens to me.
So I try to maintain this page as a tracker of some of the best open models in multiple areas, as well as 
those with novel, distinct approaches that remain usable.


## Embeddings for retrieval and more
- Whatever is on top of the relevant [Massive Text Embedding Benchmark leaderboard](https://leaderboard.mteb.org/).
- embedding-benchmark's [Retrieval Embedding Benchmark] (should be roughly equivalent to MTEB's English RTEB).
- Late interaction models are kind of awkward to score in some benchmarks and somewhat misunderstood yet, but I am a firm believer in them from the first time I laid eyes on ColBERT. 

## Reranking
- https://huggingface.co/jinaai/jina-reranker-v3
- Late interaction is also great at reranking
